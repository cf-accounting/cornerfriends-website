Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

At Corner Friends, we believe that every corner of the world can be better when compassionate people come together with purpose.

🎯

Mission Statement

At Corner Friends, our mission is to extend compassion and care to the often-overlooked corners of our society. We are a grassroots charity organisation committed to serving the vulnerable — including orphaned children and the elderly — by providing them with resources that nurture their minds, bodies, and spirits.

Through meaningful acts such as book donations, meal distributions, and emotional support, we strive to restore dignity, ignite hope, and build lasting human connections.

🌟

Vision Statement

We envision a world where no one is left behind — a world where every child has the opportunity to learn, and every elder feels valued and remembered.

Our vision is to create a community built on kindness, where acts of generosity are not just occasional gestures, but a way of life. Through continued outreach and growing partnerships, Corner Friends aims to inspire a ripple effect of compassion that reaches every forgotten corner of society.

"We believe that even small acts of kindness can spark lasting change."
— Corner Friends
Our Story So Far

Our Journey

Since our founding, Corner Friends has been actively involved in initiatives that bring tangible support and emotional warmth to those who need it most.


Every visit, every meal, every book donated — it is more than a transaction. It is a reminder to someone that the world has not forgotten them. That is what drives us forward, every single day.

Book Donations to Orphanages

We have donated educational books, stationery, and learning materials to orphanages, helping children nurture their curiosity and continue their education. Our aim is to empower these young minds to dream beyond their circumstances.

🍱

Meal Drives for Old Age Homes

We've organised and delivered hot, nutritious meals to elderly residents in old age homes, many of whom lack family support. Our visits go beyond food — we offer companionship, conversations, and the reassurance that they are not alone.

🤝

Volunteer Engagement

With the help of kind-hearted volunteers, we've been able to expand our reach, organise drives, and share moments of joy and dignity with underserved communities across our region.

Looking Ahead

What's Next

We're just getting started. Here is what Corner Friends is planning in the coming months and years.

01

Expand Our Network

Reach more orphanages and old age homes across the region, building lasting partnerships with institutions that share our values.

02

Reading Programs & Mentorship

Launch structured reading programs and mentorship sessions for children in orphanages — nurturing curiosity and building confidence.

03

Wellness Kits

Provide regular wellness kits including hygiene items and seasonal essentials to elderly residents and children in need.

04

Community Events

Develop engagement events that bring generations together — creating bonds between the young and old that last well beyond each visit.
